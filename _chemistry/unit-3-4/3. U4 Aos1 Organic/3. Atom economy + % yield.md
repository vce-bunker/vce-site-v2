atom economy - how effectively a chemical reaction incorporates reactant atoms into the desired product, minimising waste.
* molar masses from desired products (not byproducts) out of molar mass of reactants

% waste (% atom economy)
- 100%-%atom economy

% yield
- amount of desired product obtained in a chemical reaction
- never 100% due to loss of reactant material when transferring between containers
- actual grams of product / theoretical grams of product (given the actual mass of the reactant)
