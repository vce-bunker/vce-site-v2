**Explain how the electrical charge in artifical photosynthesis processes is balanced given that electrons only flow in one direction**
- E- are continually generated at the anode and used at the cathode, hence maintaining the charge balance
- H+ ions are continually generated at the anode and flow to the cathode where they are removed, hence maintaining the charge balance.

**Why can NaCl and CaCl2 be used to lower the melting point og MgCl2 but ZnCl2 cannot be used?**
NaCl and calcium chloride can be used because Na+ and CL2+(l) are weaker oxidising agents than Mg2+(l), Mg(l) will still be produced. ZnCl2 cannot be used because Zn2+ is a stronger oxidising agent than Mg2+(l) and therefore preferentially reduces to form Zn(l), not Mg(l).
